Source and licences

The Synthesis Lab runs entirely in your browser. Your files are not uploaded anywhere.

Yosys

  • Yosys 0.70, the open synthesis suite, including the ABC logic optimiser: ISC licence (ABC: permissive Berkeley licence).
  • WebAssembly build from YoWASP (@yowasp/yosys 0.70.1258), ISC licence. The files are served gzip-compressed and unpacked in your browser, unchanged.

Diagrams

  • Viz.js 3.31 (MIT), which contains Graphviz compiled to WebAssembly (Eclipse Public License 2.0), unmodified.

Lab designs and libraries

  • Lab RTL, Yosys scripts and quizzes: © vlsideepdive (Vikas Sachdeva). Free to use for learning.
  • mycells.lib and cmos_cells.lib: small demonstration libraries from the Yosys examples (ISC).
  • ibex_rtl.v: the lowRISC ibex RISC-V core (Apache-2.0), converted from SystemVerilog to Verilog-2005 with sv2v.
  • sky130_fd_sc_hd__tt_025C_1v80_synth.lib: SkyWater SKY130 HD standard cells (Apache-2.0), with cells not meant for synthesis left out.
