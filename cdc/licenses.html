The CDC Lab runs entirely in your browser. Your files are not uploaded anywhere.

CDC checker

  • The CDC checker is written by vlsideepdive for teaching. It reads the netlist that Yosys writes and a subset of SDC, and finds the common structural CDC problems. It is not a signoff CDC tool.
